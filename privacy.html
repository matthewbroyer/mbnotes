mbnotes.online

Privacy

Effective 2026-10-13

mbnotes.online is a free notes app that runs in your browser. It has no accounts and no sign-in. There are no analytics, ads or tracking scripts, and the app doesn't load anything from other websites.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved in your browser's storage on this device. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it. It isn't encrypted, so anyone who can use this browser profile can read it; avoid storing passwords or other secrets in notes.

  • mbnotes_notes_v3: your notes, checklists, tags, colors and any files you attach (stored as text inside the note)
  • mbnotes_draft: a note you were still writing when you left the page, so it can be restored; removed once it's saved
  • mbnotes-theme: whether you chose light or dark mode
  • mbnotes_has_visited: remembers that the sample notes were already added, so they aren't added again
  • mbnotes_last_backup: the date you last exported a backup, shown in Settings

Clearing your browser's site data, or using the app's own delete option (Settings → Delete all notes), removes it. If you install the app, your browser also keeps a copy of the app's files (not your data) so it opens offline.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing. The app makes no requests to other services. Links inside your notes only open when you click them, in a new tab.

Our web host receives the standard request information every website receives (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Exports and backups

Export backup downloads a .json file of your notes to your device. Where it goes after that is up to you. Import backup and Import from Google Keep read files you choose (including a Google Takeout .zip) inside your browser; nothing is uploaded. Share images are drawn on your device and only go where you send them.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
